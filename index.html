Software

Bring in work from the tools you already use

Keep drawing, retouching and checking where you are comfortable. Folio handles the layout and the export.

Cover art and photos

Adobe Photoshop

Place flattened TIFF or JPEG artwork on your cover and interior. Preflight flags any image under 300 ppi at its printed size.

Interior layouts

Adobe InDesign

Bring over text and paragraph styles from an existing interior, then match its trim size, margins and gutter in a Folio template.

Logos and ornaments

Adobe Illustrator

Use SVG or PDF vector art for imprint logos, chapter ornaments and spine text so they stay sharp at any size.

Ebook checks and conversion

Calibre

Open your exported EPUB in Calibre to preview it, edit metadata and convert it to other ebook formats.

Adobe Photoshop, InDesign and Illustrator are products of Adobe. Calibre is a separate open-source program. Folio is a sample product and is not affiliated with either.
